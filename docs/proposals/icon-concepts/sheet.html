Ten icon concepts, and the 16 px test

A capability test, not a proposal: ten candidate marks drawn as flat SVG, all in the app’s dusk-plum room with the lamp #efb768 and the page #f1e3c8. Each card shows the icon at 256, 64 and 16 px, the favicon and the icon at 32 px on a light and on a dark page. The current mark is Lamp over the page (option 6); none of these is applied to the app.

The ten

01

Single-stroke B

a letterform drawn as three fat strokes, no frame

2026 — one letterform, the strokes overlapping

02

Block B

the letter cut into flat blocks, a bright colour for the bowls

2049 — flat blocks, one bright colour per block, no gradients

03

Lit counter

a drawn B with one counter filled with the lamp

2035 — the mark supplying the colour on its own

04

Open book

the object alone: one silhouette with a valley at the spine

— no trick; the founding object, with the lamp removed

05

Turned corner

a page with its top corner folded back, the fold in the lamp

2110 — colour only in the detail, the rest held

06

Ribbon

the bookmark alone: solid, with a swallowtail notch

— no trick; silhouette only

07

Book as window

a cream page whose centre is a hole onto the lamp

2058 — the object itself set into the form

08

Two forms meeting

an abstract pair, page-half and lamp-half, closing a ring

2026 — two strokes meeting, the seam is the idea

09

Spine and boards

a book from above: two cream boards, the lit spine between

2049 — flat blocks that read as one object

10

Enclosure

the ribbon held in a hairline rounded rect, one face lit

2110 — the mark inside a rounded-rect enclosure

Every SVG here is a flat mark in the house grammar: <title> first, paths and rects, gradients in <defs> only, no filter, no image, no external reference. The sources are <slug>/icon.svg (512) and <slug>/favicon.svg (32); this page inlines them as symbols so it carries no asset of its own. Order follows the folder names.
