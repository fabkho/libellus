Three ways to place the numbers

Each file is the card with the order you asked for: cover, title, author, and then the numbers around the progress line. No dot, no “p.”, no pace line. Every file carries the same seven states (A mid-read, B percent only, C 0 %, D “The end.”, E +24, F German at 360 px, L the Library tab) in both themes.

At 360 px the text column is 202 px wide and “Aktualisieren” alone takes 104 px of it. A and B keep the numbers on one row with the action and each pays for it differently; D takes the count out of that row altogether.

Option A

One row, exactly as asked — and it breaks in German

Percent at 20 px, count at 11 px, Update at the right edge, no wrap allowed. Right in every English state and 151 px tall. In German the count is pushed under the button — the two collide instead of making room. Kept visible as the honest baseline, not as a recommendation.

Option B

One row that is allowed to wrap

The same row with wrap on, so the button drops under the numbers instead of over them when the language needs the room. Nothing overlaps or truncates. The cost is height in German: two lines on every card, 164 px, and the cover stops spanning them.

Option D

The count above the bar, small; the percent alone below it

The new one. The count sits above the progress line at 10 px — about half the size of the author beside it — so the bar reads as “126 of 300” before you have even read the number. The percent then stands alone under the bar at 20 px, with Update at the right edge. Nothing shares the button's row, so German cannot collide and the card holds 151 px in every state in both languages.
